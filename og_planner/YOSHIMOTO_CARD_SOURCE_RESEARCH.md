# Yoshimoto morphology card source research

Last updated: 2026-07-13

## Purpose

This file records source evidence for the wording on Ronald H. Yoshimoto's
physical morphology cards. It prevents a general dictionary meaning from being
mistaken for an exact card transcription.

Evidence labels used here:

- **Physical-card verified**: transcribed by the teacher from the 2007 card.
- **Exact attributed match**: the same meaning appears verbatim in a free public
  resource that explicitly attributes its cards/list to Ron Yoshimoto.
- **Component-source match**: a free reference contains the same individual
  senses, but not necessarily Yoshimoto's exact compressed wording.
- **Related only**: linguistically relevant, but insufficient evidence for card
  wording.

## Primary Yoshimoto material found locally

The project resources contain the original scanned handout
`OG/Morphology USB files/Morphology - SuffixMatching_Sorting.pdf`, headed
"by Ronald Yoshimoto, M.Ed., M.S.W., Fellow, AOGPE 2011".

Page 2 prints these direct matches:

| Suffix | Printed Yoshimoto meaning |
|---|---|
| `-able` | able to |
| `-cian` | person who |
| `-less` | without |
| `-tion` | act of / state of |
| `-ive` | related to / like |
| `-ful` | full of / having |
| `-ment` | state of / act of |
| `-ed` | past tense |
| `-ize` | make |

Page 3 prints:

| Suffix | Printed Yoshimoto meaning |
|---|---|
| `-ist` | person who |
| `-fy` | make |
| `-ly` | like / manner |
| `-ish` | resembling / origin |
| `-ing` | present tense |
| `-ess` | feminine |
| `-ible` | able to |
| `-ism` | belief / doctrine |
| `-er` | thing / person |
| `-sion` | state of / act of |

This is primary evidence for Yoshimoto's wording, although it is a 2011
matching activity rather than the complete 2007 physical card deck. It gives
an exact primary-source match for the teacher's `-able: able to` card.

Two further local scans were checked. The 50-page `6 x Mini Morph Cards` PDF is
a collection of morphology and spelling games, not the full definition-card
deck. The 227-page `Morphology.pdf` course workbook confirms in its scope and
sequence that `-age`, `-ancy/-ance`, and `-ation` are Yoshimoto suffixes, but
that page does not print their meanings.

Only physical-card verified meanings are canonical. An exact attributed match
may corroborate them. Component-source and related-only entries must never
overwrite them.

## Confirmed physical-card fingerprint

| Suffix | Physical-card meaning | Keyword | Current public evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `-able` | able to | fixable | **Exact attributed match.** The free Quizlet set “OG Morphology Suffixes, Set 1--Ron Yoshimoto” gives `-able` as “able to”. |
| `-ac` | related to | — | **Component-source match.** Webster's New World gives “Of, relating to.” |
| `-ade` | action, fruit drink | — | **Component-source match.** Webster's New World separately gives “The act of ____ing” and “Drink made from the juice of.” |
| `-age` | relating to, state of, result of | damage | **Component-source match.** Webster's New World gives “Act, condition, or result of”; its etymology note gives “belonging to, related to”. No free public source found with the complete card wording on one line. |
| `-al` | relating to | animal | **Component-source match.** American Heritage gives “Of, relating to, or characterized by”; ABC Education independently lists `-al` as “Relating to”. |
| `-an` | relating to, person | Mexican | **Very strong component-source match.** Webster's New World gives “Belonging to or having some relation to” and “Born in or living in”, with *Mexican* as its example. |
| `-ancy` | quality, state of | — | **Very strong component-source match.** Webster's New World routes `-ancy` to `-ance`, where it gives “The quality or state of being.” |
| `-ation` | state of, result of | adaptation | **Component-source match.** Webster's New World gives “The condition of being ____ed” and “The result of ____ing.” |

The eight entries form a useful fingerprint. No free public list has yet been
found that reproduces all eight as exact one-line card transcriptions. The
Webster's New World/American Heritage entries exposed freely through
YourDictionary are currently the only single reference family found that
accounts for every component sense, including the unusual two-sense `-ade` and
the *Mexican* example for `-an`. This is evidence of a likely source family, not
proof that Yoshimoto copied that edition or wording directly.

## Strong pre-2007 source-family candidate

Michael J. Sheehan's *Word Parts Dictionary: Standard and Reverse Listings of
Prefixes, Suffixes, Roots and Combining Forms* (McFarland, 2000; ISBN
0-7864-0819-7) is the closest single pre-2007 source found so far. Its
alphabetical entries reproduce the unusual combinations behind seven of the
eight teacher-confirmed cards:

| Card | Sheehan 2000 entry | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| `-able: able to` | first sense is `able to` | Exact wording |
| `-ac: related to` | `of; pertaining to` | Direct synonym |
| `-ade: action, fruit drink` | `the act of`; `drink made from` | Same two unusual senses |
| `-age: relating to, state of, result of` | Dictionary: `act; condition; result of`; reverse Finder also indexes `-age` under `relating to` and `result (of)` | Near-exact reconstruction of the whole card |
| `-al: relating to` | `belonging to; like; of` | Direct semantic match |
| `-an: relating to, person` | `belonging to; of; characteristic of`; `born in; living in` | Same adjective/person split |
| `-ancy: quality, state of` | cross-reference through `-ance`: `quality or state of being` | Exact core wording |
| `-ation: state of, result of` | Reverse Finder indexes `-ation` under both `condition` and `result (of)` | Exact semantic reconstruction |

The dictionary predates the 2007 cards and places all these uncommon entries
next to one another in its A-section. More importantly, its reverse Finder
explains wording that ordinary dictionary entries did not: `-age` is explicitly
indexed under `relating to`, `condition`, and `result (of)`, while `-ation` is
indexed under `condition` and `result (of)`. Yoshimoto's compact cards therefore
look consistent with selected Finder categories translated into classroom
language (`condition` -> `state`). This is a materially stronger source-family
match than unrelated modern teaching lists. It is still not proof of direct
copying, and the teacher's physical cards remain canonical.

## Other traced sources

- Michael McKinnon's 502-page card product says it is “Based on the list
  compiled by Ron Yoshimoto.” Its public preview confirms the colour system and
  scope, but does not expose a readable suffix list. It is paid and must not be
  treated as a free source:
  <https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Greek-Latin-Morphology-Cards-Orton-Gillingham-3608860>
- Speech Pathology Tasmania publicly indexes both “Suffixes Matching and
  Sorting” and “Morphology - Suffix Matching Sorting” under its advanced
  morphology alumni resources. The files are access-controlled and no older
  legitimate free copy was found in the Wayback Machine:
  <https://spt.com.au/alumni-resource/suffixes-matching-and-sorting/>
- William Van Cleave's free *Morphology Matters* handout contains science,
  mathematics and social-science morpheme tables explicitly labelled “Ron
  Yoshimoto compilation”. These are strong evidence for many roots, but they do
  not reproduce the suffix-card deck:
  <https://onlit.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Morphology-Matters-Building-Vocabulary-Through-Word-Parts-van-Cleave.pdf>

## Rejected as exact deck sources

The following are useful linguistic references but do not reproduce the full
eight-card fingerprint and therefore are not exact card sources:

- Merriam-Webster's free *Dictionary of Prefixes, Suffixes, and Combining
  Forms*.
- The free “Greek and Latin Suffixes” table circulated by Los Angeles Mission
  College and mirrors of that table.
- *The Reading Teacher's Book of Lists* suffix tables.
- NSW Department of Education spelling-sequence suffix tables.

## Operational rule

When generating an OG lesson:

1. Use a teacher-verified physical-card meaning verbatim when present in
   `morpheme_bank.json`.
2. Use this research file to report corroboration and provenance, not to replace
   card wording.
3. If only a component-source meaning exists, label it **reference-only**.
4. Do not infer the rest of the physical deck merely because one dictionary
   family explains the known examples.
