## What the style is, read off the examples

- Tick box descriptors: four or five per cohort per semester, never six in any of the three years. Each opens with a present-tense verb (Recognises, Uses, Engages, Participates, Follows, Identifies, Applies, Sustains, Communicates). Each names the activity, game, text or video in brackets so a parent can picture it (Mix and Mingle, Kahoot, Detective, Sally and Possum, El Deafo, Map Challenge). Foundation and Discovery statements often end with a support qualifier: with support, with prompting, with guidance. Challenge and Enrichment statements drop the qualifier and add a fluency or duration claim (increasing fluency, beyond 5 minutes, with growing accuracy). Cultural statements sit alongside language statements in every cohort. From 2025 Semester 2 the descriptors are numbered 1 to 5.
- General statement: one paragraph of 80 to 110 words per cohort per semester, past tense, opening In Semester N, <Cohort> students..., walking through the terms in order, naming the texts, videos, games and showcase task, and closing on a cultural or strategic learning rather than a vocabulary list.
- A unit therefore supplies: its four to six tick box descriptors in this voice, and a 40 to 60 word term contribution paragraph in the general-statement voice that Chris merges with the other term's when he writes the semester report.
