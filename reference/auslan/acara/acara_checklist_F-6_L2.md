# ACARA F-6 (L2 sequence) Auslan Skills and Knowledge Checklist, as supplied by Chris

Filed 17 September 2026 from reference/auslan/acara/Auslan-Checklist-ACARA-F-6-sequence-L2.docx. Wording untouched, converted to ASCII. Paste this file into the unit prompt's ACARA checklist input.

What it is: five skill and knowledge statements per year level, Foundation to Year 6, each rated per term on a three-level scale: E = Emerging, C = Consolidating, P = Proficient. The last row of each year (row 4 for Foundation) is the cultural strand. Row references used elsewhere in the pipeline are Year.Row, for example Y4.2 = Uses CA, DS, and NMFs to retell familiar stories and describe characters.

How Chris wants it used: the three levels are the rubric scale for every assessment piece and every journal self-assessment, regardless of the student's year level, so a Year 2 who signs better than a Year 6 is rated against the same rows and the rating means the same thing. It also sets the cultural knowledge level for Foundation and Discovery.
