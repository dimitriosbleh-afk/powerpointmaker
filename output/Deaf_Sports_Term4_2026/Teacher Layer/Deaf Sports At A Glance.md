**Deaf Sports in Australia | Challenge and Enrichment | Term 4 2026 | 8 lessons of 50 minutes**

**Unit anchor: Eyes up. Watch. Copy.** Same words in every I Do. 8 teaching lessons against 7 usable weeks, so two lessons share a week or the review runs once: that decision is still open. All evidence complete by week 7. Reports written week 9.

| **Lesson** | **Topic** | **Vocab** | **Learning intention and success criteria** | **Activities** | **Assessment and materials** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Finding the signs you need | None new. Every example is a sport sentence, so the 12 sport signs are met in passing | **LI** Find which words in my sentence need a sign, and look them up.<br>**SC** Underline the words that need a sign.<br>Find one in Sign It! and give its page.<br>Sign it to my partner, book closed. | Underline together, then find a page<br>Race the index if there is time | Exit rotation, group 1, looking-up skill<br>Sign It! per pair, boards, journals |

| 3 | Who, what, where | WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHAT MEAN?, PRACTISE | **LI** Ask who, what and where, and ask for an explanation.<br>**SC** Ask one of the three profile questions.<br>Ask all three and record the answers.<br>Use WHAT MEAN? when a repeat would not help. | 🎲 4 Find Your Team | Exit rotation, group 2<br>Team Role Cards, boards |

**Weeks 8 and 9.** The review lesson, run twice, nothing new taught and no evidence collected. Learning intention: use everything from this term under time pressure. Week 8 runs games 1, 6 and 8 with the timeline and profile cards. Week 9 runs games 2, 5 and 7 with the medal tally sheets and boards. Points sheet on the board both weeks. A relief teacher runs it from the CRT Review Pack.

**Differentiation, lesson by lesson**

| **Lesson** | **🪜 Support** | **🚀 Extend** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The word chosen for them, so the task is finding and signing only | Look up a second word and sign both in one go |
| 2 | Three-sport picture card on the desk, so the answer is a point plus a sign | Find a sport nobody else named, and say how you found that person |
| 3 | The three question words in English, face up, so the load is the sign not the sequence | Ask a fourth question of your own and explain why you chose it |
| 4 | Number line on the desk, so a guess is a point plus a sign | More or Less with years only, 1880 to 2026 |
| 5 | The three cards face up in the correct order: reading, not recall | Recount three events and add what changed because of the middle one |
| 6 | Three picture prompts, a pool light, a flag and a scoreboard: choose and explain | A few extra seconds of explaining: find one place in this room where it would do the same job |
| 7 | Prompt card face up for the recording, marked supported on the checklist | Interview a partner in role as a person from a profile card |
| 8 | The story as four pictures, with the two swimmers already in different positions | Retell from the second swimmer's point of view |
| Review | Pair an unsure student with a secure one and let them answer as one | Play the round with the cards face down |

**Band calibration.** Challenge asks two questions with the card visible, works to 50, and retells two events. Enrichment asks four unprompted plus a follow-up, works to 100, and retells all four.

**8 lessons, 7 weeks.** One week carries two lessons, or the review runs once. That decision is open.

**If a lesson is lost.** Move everything one week later and run the review once, in week 9. If two go, compress Lesson 6 into the back half of Lesson 5. Never compress Lessons 7 or 8.

**Full hour available.** Lesson 1 race the index. Lesson 2 Mix and Mingle again with a new question. Lesson 3 swap partners and run the three questions again. Lesson 4 the 1880 to 2026 range as a class round. Lesson 5 add timeline cards 9 and 10. Lesson 6 walk the classroom barrier hunt. Lesson 7 each pair watches their recording once. Lesson 8 pairs film the retell into their journal.
