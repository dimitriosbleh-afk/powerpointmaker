## Decisions from the 17 September slide review

| Decision | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| A new Lesson 1 | The Do Now asks students to pick which words need a sign and look them up, and none of that is obvious. It is now taught as its own lesson, with every example a sport sentence so the practice doubles as first exposure to the term's vocabulary. Everything after it shifts by one, so the unit is now 8 teaching lessons. **Open: what gives.** Evidence still has to be complete by the reporting deadline, so either the unit runs to week 8 and the review lesson runs once, or two of the old lessons merge |
| The anchor | Changed from "Set it up, ask it, watch it, check it" to **"Eyes up. Watch. Copy."** Chris: the interview process is not used for weeks, so anchoring to it teaches something nobody needs yet. The fourth card is the attention move: tap or wave, then point, never saying a name out loud. These four are signed and copied, because responding to signed classroom instructions is curriculum |
| Copy once | Not six times. The teacher models twice, students copy once, and the repetitions come from using the sign in the We Do and You Do |
| Lesson 2 We Do | Bob Virus is out of this lesson. Students cannot ask each other about sport before they have found and practised the sport signs, so the We Do is eight minutes with Sign It! and the GIFs, in pairs, checking each other. The game returns once the vocabulary is in |
| Whiteboard routine | **Hover it, chin it on three.** The school routine from Foundation to Year 6, from Matt's PD. The board stays flat until the cue so nobody shows early and nobody copies. "Boards up" is retired |
| Evidence from a board | Photograph the board into the journal. The photo is the evidence; the board gets wiped |
| Cultural questions | "Who uses Auslan" is too basic for Year 5 and 6. Every cultural question now ties to the unit's own content and asks why or what it means |
| Success criteria | Numbered, not bulleted. The Learning Intention and Success Criteria headings are bigger and the two cards are further apart |
| Cue icons | Coloured, one colour per cue, and larger |
| Images on vocabulary slides | Chris asked for pictures beside the twelve sports, then agreed with the house approach once it was explained: less on the screen, so the picture does not compete with the sign. **Settled: no images on vocabulary list slides** |
| Signbank agency | The pipeline goes and gets the right sign itself rather than shipping a lookup card and asking Chris to do it. It now reads the whole recorded definition and picks the variant carrying the sense the lesson needs, and Chris overrides any of it from `reference/auslan/signbank_links/overrides.json` |
