=== SLIDE 10 ===
  TEXT | Read pages 1-21 of The Very Cranky Bear
  IMG  | Google Shape;485;p69
  TEXT | What do you think the author wants us to learn from the story about how the animals help Bear?
  IMG  | Google Shape;487;p69
  EXISTING_NOTES |
    Before reading-
    Activate prior knowledge
    What were the biggest feelings Grace and Mr Milligan experienced in the story?
    What were the most important ways they showed care for each other?
    What have we learned about being a good neighbour?
    What do you think the story was really trying to teach us last time we read it?
    Have students make personal connections
    Why do you think caring for others matters in your life?
    When someone shows you kindness, how does it change the way you feel or act?
    Have you ever learned something important about helping others from someone in your family or community?
    
    Guiding question
    What is the main message the author wants us to understand about caring for others?
    
    During reading-
    Check pause points
    
    After reading-
    Anticipated responses: 
    Caring is doing kind things to make someone happy, like when Grace and Mr Milligan eat fruit and go to the beach together.
    Caring is helping someone when they’re sad, like when Mr Milligan is heartbroken after Charlie dies and Grace comforts him.
    Caring is noticing how someone is feeling. Grace sees that Mr Milligan is lonely and sad and tries to help.
    Caring is gentle. Grace sits quietly with Mr Milligan when he’s upset about Charlie instead of trying to ‘fix it’.
