=== SLIDE 10 ===
  TEXT | Read pages 1-21 of The Very Cranky Bear
  IMG  | Google Shape;486;p69
  TEXT | How do the illustrations help us to understand what is going on in the story?
  IMG  | Google Shape;488;p69
  EXISTING_NOTES |
    Before reading-
    Activate prior knowledge
    What can illustrations show that words sometimes don’t tell us?
    Have you noticed anything special that Grace wears or draws in the pictures? What do you think those little details might tell us about her?
    Have students make personal connections
    Do you have objects or symbols (e.g., jewellery, art, decorations, clothing) that show something important about who you are or your family?
    
    Guiding question
    How do the pictures show us more about Grace, Mr Milligan, and their emotions or culture?
    
    During reading-
    Check pause points
    
    After reading-
    Guiding question
    How do the pictures show us more about Grace, Mr Milligan, and their emotions or culture?
    Anticipated responses: 
    The colours change to show emotions. There are bright colours for happy moments and darker colours when Charlie dies.
    The characters’ body language shows feelings, like Mr Milligan slumping when he’s grieving, and Grace holding her head in her hands.
    Grace’s necklace and drawings give clues about her culture, showing symbols that appear again in her artwork even though the words never mention it.
    The endpapers show important memories, with a photo wall showing Grace, Charlie, and Mr Milligan together, highlighting what Mr Milligan treasures.
