# Hands-on activity: 2D Shape Hunt

![](GoogleShape1394p154.jpg)
Materials: White or blackboardsn(to lean on) pencils, worksheet
Instructions:
Take boards, worksheet and pencil outside to the playground, sandpit or explore in the classroom if preferred.
Explain to students that you are recording how many circles, squares, rectangles and triangles you can find.
Students to record how many of each shape they find on the worksheet.

Notes to teacher:
To support students: Teacher to prompt students to a specific area in the playground or sand pit to obvious examples.
Extension students: Can move on to recording 2D shapes on the school building
and/or prompting them to record rhombus’, parallelograms and kites.
