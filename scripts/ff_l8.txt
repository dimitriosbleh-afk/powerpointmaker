  The F-2 Curriculum provides two options:

  Please download the accompanying Teacher Handbook that matches this unit.

  identifying which components of the lesson may need adapting, depending on the lesson time available
  pre-reading the specified pages to understand key themes, events and big ideas

As these are repeated across the unit, it is anticipated that the pace of each lesson should increase as students become more confident with newly acquired skills and knowledge.

===== SLIDE 16 =====
  Structure of a recount
  I
  On my last birthday, my family and I went to the beach.
  First ….
  Then ….
  After that ….
  It made me feel special because I spent time with my family at my favourite place.
  Image credit: Generated using AI
  --- EXISTING NOTES ---
  Revise the structure of a recount using the co-constructed example of a birthday celebration. Read the recount aloud, discussing the included parts (opening sentence, detail sentences, final sentence). As you discuss these parts, remind students of the icons that match the stages of a recount. Talk through what belongs in each part, using the icons to support understanding. Students can do the actions for each icon. 
  
  Opening sentence: (includes when, where and who, and describes the main event of the celebration)
  On my last birthday, my family and I went to the beach.
  
  Detail sentences: (uses time connectives like first, then, after that to order the events in the celebration)
  First, I played cricket with my family on the sand.
  Then, my brother and I went swimming in the ocean.
  After that, we shared and ate lots of yummy food.
  
  Final sentence: (tells the reader how you felt, and finishes the recount by stating why the celebration was special to you)
  It made me feel special because I spent time with my family at my favourite place.

===== SLIDE 38 =====
  Different adjectives for the same noun
  I
  There are often lots of possible adjectives to describe a particular noun. Each adjective will make the sentence sound a bit different, as they describe different things about the noun.
  For example, we can swap out the adjective ‘colourful’ in this sentence with another adjective to describe different things about the lion.
  The colourful lion danced.
  The ferocious lion danced
  The energetic lion danced.
