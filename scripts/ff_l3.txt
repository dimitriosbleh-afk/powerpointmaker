  Express and extended path options
  Coloured slides
  The F-2 Curriculum provides two options:
  Express Path: 30 minutes over 4 days with flexible teacher-choice focus for day 5 including optional weekly review materials for use alongside all or part of larger focus phonics programs (i.e. that include a language comprehension / writing component).
  Extended Path: 60 minutes over 4 days, including daily review, with flexible teacher-choice focus on day 5 for use alongside narrower focus phonics programs (i.e. word reading, spelling, fluency and handwriting only).
  The coloured slides within this slide deck are included in the Express Path option.

===== SLIDE 3 =====
  Use of this resource
  Please download the accompanying Teacher Handbook that matches this unit.
  Read the text and familiarise yourself with e.g. key themes, big ideas, characters, themes and literary features.
  This resource is designed to be modified and adapted to suit the individual context and needs of each school, teacher and cohort of students.
  It is recommended that teachers read the lesson detail provided in the Teacher Handbook in preparation for delivering this lesson. This may include:
  identifying which components of the lesson may need adapting, depending on the lesson time available
  pre-reading the specified pages to understand key themes, events and big ideas
  deciding on suitable pause points relevant to students comprehension abilities/needs and which queries will be asked to facilitate class discussion and overall comprehension of the text
  the incidental vocabulary that may need to be taught during reading
  any chosen literary devices that will be explored
  identifying which booklet tasks will be completed
  Learning outcomes have been designed to be repeated across a unit of work. Students may initially take longer when first introduced to new concepts and related booklet tasks. As these are repeated across the unit, it is anticipated that the pace of each lesson should increase as students become more confident with newly acquired skills and knowledge.

===== SLIDE 5 =====
  Whiteboard response
  All students respond to the teacher’s question, showing their responses at the same time.
  Non-volunteers
  The teacher asks a question, gives wait time, then calls on individual students to respond.
  Independent work
  Students respond to a prompt in their exercise book or booklet.
  Pair share
  Students discuss with the person next to them.
  Choral response
  Students respond orally all together.
  Thumbs up, thumbs down
  Students indicate whether they agree or disagree with a statement, or whether something is an example or non-example.
  Engagement icons - to go in top-right corner
  Class discussion
  Students discuss as a class and share ideas.
  Show fingers
  Students indicate using their fingers which is the correct answer. Ideal for multiple choice answers.

===== SLIDE 30 =====
  Y
  Class activity
  Sit in a circle.
  When it’s your turn, say an adjective to describe the noun in the image.
  What colour is it? What shape is it? What size is it?
  Put the adjective into a sentence when describing the noun.
  --- EXISTING NOTES ---
  Students sit in a large circle. Display a picture (or hold up an object) related to the text. Students take turns going around the circle, saying an adjective for the item. Change to a new item once adjectives for the item have been exhausted.
  
  Options for nouns have been provided - use as many or as little as you like, or swap for objects or other images.
