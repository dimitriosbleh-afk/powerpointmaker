  The F-2 Curriculum provides two options:

  Please download the accompanying Teacher Handbook that matches this unit.

  identifying which components of the lesson may need adapting, depending on the lesson time available

As these are repeated across the unit, it is anticipated that the pace of each lesson should increase as students become more confident with newly acquired skills and knowledge.

  Adjectives
  I
  An adjective is a word that tell us more about a noun - a person, place or thing. It describes what a noun is like.
  Adjectives can give us more information about the size, colour, shape, age or quality of a noun.

===== SLIDE 32 =====
  How do we choose the best adjective?
  I
  To help us choose an adjective to describe a noun, we can ask ourselves some questions:
  What colour is it?
  What does it look like?
  How does the person or thing feel?
  What shape or size is it?
  What might it feel like if you touched it?
  --- EXISTING NOTES ---
  Explain that when choosing an adjective, we can consider the features of the noun, such as: 
  Size (eg. big)
  Shape (eg. round)
  Colour (eg. blue)
  Visual aspects (eg. pointy)
  Texture (eg. fluffy)
  Feelings (eg. happy)
