===== SLIDE 38 =====
  Y
  Class activity: match the adjective to the noun
  When you hear an adjective, walk to the picture of a noun that best matches that adjective.
  Then, think of a sentence that includes both the adjective and noun.
  e.g. If you hear the adjective ‘sparkly’, you might walk to the picture of the firecrackers, and your sentence could be ‘The sparkly firecrackers made a bang.’
  --- EXISTING NOTES ---
  Display large pictures/posters of nouns from Fang Fang’s Chinese New Year around the room: lantern, dragon, firecrackers, Chinese lion, chopsticks, dumplings etc. (see booklet for images of nouns to print)
  Call out an adjective, e.g., red. (see next slide for list of adjectives)
  Students walk to the picture that best matches the adjective (e.g., lantern).
  After each turn, say a simple sentence using the adjective and noun.
  Check understanding:
  Why did you choose this picture?
  Does the adjective fit the noun?
  
  Optional scaffolds: Offer two adjectives and ask students to choose the better fit: Is the restaurant ‘busy’ or ‘quiet’?
  Optional extension: Students suggest another adjective for the same noun and repeat the sentence orally.
