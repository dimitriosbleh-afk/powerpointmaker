[TEXT] Review & Reflect
[TEXT] Turn & Talk
[TEXT] What Did We Learn?
[TEXT] Key Takeaways
[TEXT] •  SC1: I can count money in different denominations up to $20
[TEXT] •  SC2: I can compare the amount of money I have to the cost
[TEXT] •  SC3: I can decide whether I can afford something

[NOTES] SAY:
- Let's check our success criteria
- SC1: I can count money in different denominations. Thumbs?
- SC2: I can compare my money to the cost of something. Thumbs?
- SC3: I can decide whether I can afford something. Thumbs?
- Turn and talk: When counting money, which denomination do you start with? Why?

DO:
- Display SC on screen
- Run thumbs check for each
- Allow 30 seconds for Turn and Talk
- Cold call 2-3 students: "Start with the biggest because it is easier to add on."

TEACHER NOTES:
The Turn and Talk targets the counting strategy (start with largest denomination). This is the key takeaway for efficient money counting.

WATCH FOR:
- Students who say "start with the biggest" -- they have the strategy
- Students who are unsure -- they may need revisiting in future lessons
